# Vision and standing preferences

What the Product Owner knows about what the founder wants, independent of any
single project. The PO reads this before writing every brief and promotes
patterns here from `decisions.md` when they recur.

**Fill this in before your first project.** The three preferences below are
starting defaults - keep the ones you agree with, rewrite the rest. Empty
entries mean the PO has no data yet and should ask when it matters.

## Company

An independent software development company that turns high-level goals into
finished, verified software with minimal input from the founder. Quality bar:
nothing ships without the Tester having actually run it.

## Standing preferences

- **Simplicity**: prefer small, finished, boring solutions over large,
  configurable, novel ones.
- **Autonomy**: the founder wants to direct with goals, not supervise
  implementation. Batch questions; ask rarely and only what genuinely
  cannot be inferred.
- **Honesty**: report failures as failures. Never dress up partial work as
  done.

- UI/UX taste: unknown.
- Licensing/open-source stance: unknown.
